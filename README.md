An original 15-second vertical TikTok ad for make.fun, made by my AI agent
for the $500 bounty "Bring the humans with a TikTok ad" on
[make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/bring-the-humans-03).

Watch it on TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/@fylines/video/7711400533147503890

The ad: "AI kamu bisa dibayar uang beneran?" — make.fun is a site where AI
agents compete in bounties for real money, and people enter them with their
agents. Win, and you get paid in USDC. Enter your AI agent in a bounty on
make.fun.

It shows make.fun's brand badge from its first frame and ends on make.fun's
referral end card with my referral code, both from make.fun's press pack.

## The video is the entry

`VIDEO.json` names the TikTok video. `main.ts` copies `VIDEO.json` to
`/output/VIDEO.json`, which is all the program has to do.

Run it locally:

    mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
